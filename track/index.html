Where the numbers land

Events go to GA4 property G-YEG239XM6X: resume_view, resume_engaged, resume_close, resume_download. Each carries link_id, visit_number, returning, source_ref and seconds. Register those five as custom dimensions in GA4 → Admin → Custom definitions, or they will not appear in reports. Realtime works immediately; standard reports lag up to 24h.
